## Target

Caricature NPC models from `docs/v2/visual/art/npcs.md` and `Config/NpcArchetypes.luau` —
Pak RT, Tengkulak, Polisi Desa, and the debt collector — swapped for the box NPCs behind

party or slogan (the Polisi "badge" is a plain fictional disc).

## Approach change (recorded)

The queue suggested building in Blender or with Studio mesh tools and importing, at R15 scale.
Two constraints blocked that: subagent transport was down throughout (`ECONNREFUSED`), and place
saves are forbidden on this branch. Instead the caricature detail pieces are **procedural,
source-controlled Roblox primitive parts** behind an off-by-default `npcs` LookDev candidate:
durable in Rojo, reversible, no place save and no asset IDs. They attach to the existing
`NpcFactory` box NPCs (`Head` / `Torso`) with local-space CFrame offsets, so they follow the
`Model:PivotTo` walk loop for free. This is a LookDev candidate, not a redesign of shipping NPCs.

## What was made

- `src/shared/NpcModelCandidates.luau` — pure data, no asset IDs: the 8-token palette and 19
  detail recipes (Pak RT 6: tall peci, two-part moustache, clipboard; Tengkulak 5: heavy brow,
  sling strap + pouch, hunch; Polisi 4: oversized cap, fictional badge, belt; debt collector 4:
  top hat, satchel, cold sash). `recipeFor(id)` maps the archetype ids and the name-based
  `DebtCollector` alias.
- `src/client/World/LookDevNpcs.luau` — the client applier. It decorates every `NpcSpawn`-tagged
  model and any `DebtCollector` by name, watches for late spawns, and destroys every created
  instance on disable. Shape resolution is best-effort (see Studio note below). Nothing is
  parented into the place save; the details exist only while the candidate is enabled in a
  Studio session.
- `LookDev.luau` gains a fifth pure-data `npcs` candidate (`idsSource` pointer, no numeric IDs),
  wired at boot in `Main.client.luau` behind `pcall(require, …)`.

**Restricted-VM note.** The Studio MCP `execute_luau` sandbox has no `Enum.PartShape` member
(`PartShape is not a valid member of "Enum"`), which initially aborted `apply()`. Shape
resolution is now under `pcall`: on failure the Part keeps Roblox's default Block shape, so the
details still attach and the toggle is fully proven. The real LocalScript VM resolves
Cylinder/Ball/Wedge. `selene`'s Roblox std also omits `PartShape`, so the enum is indexed
dynamically to keep lint clean.

## Render

`docs/v2/visual/art/npcs/render/contact_sheet.png` is generated by
`docs/v2/visual/art/npcs/src/generate_npc_preview.py`, which parses the palette and all 19
recipes **directly from `NpcModelCandidates.luau`** and draws a front-elevation schematic per
NPC (box body, each piece at its X/Y offset, labelled legend with shape / palette token /
offsets). It is a review aid derived from the shipped recipe data, **not a Studio screenshot**:
this session's `screen_capture` produced an image that could not be saved to disk or viewed, so
the live enable/disable proof above is the evidence of record. No agent visually graded the art;
the schematic is for the designer's yes/no only.
