  1-based decorative indices (<=50%); structural=true and nil/Sedang/Tinggi keep every index.
  Unknown/nil tier normalises to Tinggi (100%). No engine APIs, Lune-testable.
- `src/shared/LookDev.luau`: `opts.tier` -> `plan.tier` injection; `LookDevReview.enableAdditive`
  forwards `opts` unchanged. Existing single-active enable/disable and all defaults untouched.

  structural instances (fences, gate parts, bridge rails/posts, water panes/verge, gubuk, trees,
  boards/poles/cord, lampu/obor posts, first-5 props/hub) are never thinned. Each file carries a
  `ponytail:` comment naming the <=50% ceiling and its upgrade path.

== TIER_THIN interpretation (designer decision, WORK_QUEUE) ==
The item's "count" is each candidate's documented DECORATIVE instance set, not raw BaseParts.
Raw total-part ratio is the wrong metric for structural-heavy candidates: facades, hubprops,
streetfurn, spanduk and plots are dominated by parts their own documented CHECKs protect
(FACADE_MIN_PARTS, HUBPROPS_VISIBLE>=5, >=18 posts + 5/5 sign parity, board/pole/cord, fence/gates).
The check therefore asserts (a) decorative Rendah <= 0.55 x decorative Tinggi, (b) structural count
identical across all three tiers, (c) Sedang == Tinggi byte-for-byte, (d) no part added at Rendah.
