== ONE REAL ERROR FOUND AND FIXED (not allowlisted or hidden) ==
First stage attempt built only 85/91 parts: 6 `toga_leaf_*` parts warned
`[LookDevLumbungR8] could not stage part <house>_toga_leaf_<n> for <house>`. Root cause: those recipes
used material token `Leaf`, which (like the earlier `Painted` bug) is NOT a member of Enum.Material in
this Studio even though `LookDev.BUILTIN_MATERIALS` lists it. FIXED: lumbung's `leaf`/`herb` material
tokens -> `Plastic` (green read still comes from Color3), with a spec guard forbidding both stale
tokens. The shared allowlist entry was deliberately LEFT in place: `LookDevCorner` and `ModelMapPlazaR9`
also consume `Leaf`, so removing it would be out-of-scope collateral. A fresh Play then staged all 91
parts (90 architecture + 1 gauge) with ZERO warnings.
