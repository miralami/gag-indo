== Safe-area finding: ONE plain bug (decorative shadow), not a text/layout change ==
The lone violation is `AdminPanel.PanelShadow`, an auto-generated decoration from `Theme.dropShadow` (src/client/UI/Theme.luau:135-147), NOT an interactive element and NOT text. Root cause: `Admin.luau:204` parents the refit `UIScale` to `frame` (the Panel) only. `PanelShadow` is a SIBLING of `Panel` under the AdminPanel ScreenGui, so the scale is not applied to it; on short landscape the panel shrinks to fit but the full-size shadow stays and escapes the safe area (y=-182..508 vs safe y=-58..317). At 375x812 and 1024x768 no shrink is needed (scale=1) so the shadow fits — hence the single landscape violation.

Live fix probe (812x375): reparenting the UIScale from `frame` to the ScreenGui (parent of both Panel and PanelShadow) produced Panel bounds IDENTICAL (334,12,478,305 before and after) and pulled the shadow INSIDE (240,-182,580,508 -> 336,14,480,307). This changes no designer-chosen panel size/position; it only makes the generated decoration track the existing refit. Classified as a plain anchor/scale bug — the kind the item authorises fixing — not a designer layout move. Probe restored the original parent before ending.
