== Root Cause & Fix ==
In sprint 3, shots #01, #02, #03, #13 and #14 retained character-level framing with grass filling the
lens, despite CAMERA_GRASS and SubjectCoverage reporting passes. Cause: during a Play session, the
client PlayerScripts camera controller resets workspace.CurrentCamera to Custom (character-follow)
between execution calls. Setting CurrentCamera to Scriptable once at check time reverted immediately
when the command yielded, so subsequent screen_capture calls snapped from character eye height.

Fix:
1. `src/shared/CapturePose.luau` (pure math, POSITION_TOL=0.5 stud, ANGLE_TOL_DEG=2 deg).
2. Pure Lune spec `tests/specs/capture_pose_spec.luau` (14 unit test cases, all pass).
3. Client-DM capture protocol: connect a RunService.RenderStepped hold enforcing CameraType=Scriptable
   and CFrame=CFrame.lookAt(eye, lookAt), wait two frames, measure pose via CapturePose.measure(),
   perform screen_capture, then disconnect the hold and restore prior CameraType and CFrame.
4. `tools/studio/check_q264.luau` validates applied review-camera pose (PASS) and validates non-vacuity
   by measuring character-level camera against the review camera (FAIL calibration).
