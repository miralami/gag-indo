## Placement notes (descriptive)

- **Yard edge:** pisang and bambu as dense clumps; mangga, nangka and rambutan as single canopy trees; jambu air and pepaya nearer the house; talas/keladi in wet, shady pockets.
- **Hedge line:** teh-tehan as the clipped low living fence; kembang sepatu and puring as mixed ornamental hedge near the front.
- **Fence line:** bambu as a vertical screen, puring/kembang sepatu at gaps; keep paths and gates clear.
- **Sawah edge:** singkong on slightly higher dry ground above the bund; alang-alang as rough grass on the bund and uncultivated margins.
- **In pots:** lidah mertua, and pandan where a small clump is wanted — grouped on porch or steps, not scattered in fields.
- Exclusion zones, tier budgets and which clusters are staged for the saved camera belong to the implementation item; this brief makes no visual verdict.
