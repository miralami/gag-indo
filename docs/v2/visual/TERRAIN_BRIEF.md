# Terrain brief — advice only (WORK_QUEUE item 49)

For the designer's own sculpt. No agent sculpted or dressed spawn, no terrain was changed,
no place was saved or published, and no visual was graded. These are elevation suggestions
for a gentle, believable Javanese/Sundanese village — not a build plan.

## Suggested elevation bands

- Sawah: 1-2 studs lower than the yard.
- Yards: at grade with the main paths.
- Warung and pasar: a slight rise toward both.
- Drainage ditches: along the paths.
- Advice only: no agent sculpts terrain or places anything.

## Notes for the sculpt

- Keep changes gradual: the village is mostly flat, not terraced steps at spawn.
- Let the sawah lip read as a soft edge rather than a wall; keep ditches beside paths, not
  across them.
- Where the designer raises the warung or pasar apron, check that the yard still meets the
  path at grade so doorways and plots stay usable.
- The kit in `ServerStorage.DesignerKit` is drag-source only. It has anchored, base-pivoted,
  true-to-scale items; it does not place itself or decide the look. All look verdicts stay
  with the designer.
